DropCut

by Two In Tesla

DropCut is a private tool used by the creators of the Two In Tesla channels to publish their own short vertical videos.

The creators send a video they filmed to a private Telegram bot. DropCut adjusts the playback speed, suggests a title and cover, and after the owner approves it, uploads the video to the creators' own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram at scheduled times.

DropCut is not offered to the public. It only accesses the accounts of its owners, who connect them themselves.

YouTube API Services

DropCut uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3, upload only) to publish the owners' videos to their own YouTube channel. By connecting a YouTube account, the user agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Read how DropCut handles data in our Privacy Policy; access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
