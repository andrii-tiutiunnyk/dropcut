Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

DropCut is a private publishing tool operated by Two In Tesla (contact: twointesla@gmail.com). It is used only by its owners to upload their own videos to their own social media accounts.

What data DropCut accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (scope youtube.upload). DropCut does not read, list or modify any other YouTube or Google data.
  • TikTok: basic profile info and permission to upload videos to the owner's TikTok account as drafts.
  • Facebook / Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the owner's Page and Instagram account.
  • Videos: video files the owners send to their private Telegram bot.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens and videos are stored only on the owners' own computer. They are used only to upload the owners' videos. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Video frames and short text notes may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic Claude) to suggest a title; they are not used for any other purpose.

Videos are deleted from the computer within 30 days after they are published. Discarded videos are deleted within 2 days.

Google API Services

DropCut's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. DropCut uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, and in Facebook under Settings → Business integrations. Stored tokens are then useless and are deleted on request.
